About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at Florida State University. My research interests lie in computer systems, disaggregated memory, RDMA-based systems, key-value stores, distributed systems, and high-performance computing.

I am currently working on efficient data structures and indexing mechanisms for disaggregated memory systems, with a focus on improving latency, scalability, and resource efficiency for remote-memory access workloads.

Research focus: Disaggregated memory, RDMA, key-value stores, distributed indexing, and high-performance systems.

Research Interests

  • Disaggregated memory systems and remote memory access
  • RDMA-based key-value stores and distributed indexing
  • Hybrid index design for point and range queries
  • Performance characterization of distributed systems
  • GPU parallelization and large-scale machine learning systems
